Retrieve the active cashier shift summary and register drawer status from ArunakiZen POS.

Capabilities:
- Fetches initial opening cash float (`kas_awal`).
- Computes real-time gross revenue, cash sales turnover, credit sales, and transaction count.
- Calculates the expected cash amount that should be physically present in the cash drawer (`kas_seharusnya`).
- Identifies current logged-in operator, shift number, and shift active/closed status.

When to use:
- The user asks for cashier daily recap, shift report, or cash drawer balance (e.g. "rekap kasir", "cek uang di laci", "omset shift ini", "laporan kasir").
