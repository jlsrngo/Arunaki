Adjust or correct inventory stock quantities (Stock Opname / Stock Correction) in ArunakiZen.

Capabilities:
- Records official stock adjustment document with prefix 'KS-' (e.g. 'KS-241026001').
- Records previous stock, new physical count, and stock difference in the audit trail.
- Automatically binds the active authenticated cashier identity from the user's login session.
- Updates store inventory balance atomically.

When to use:
- The user requests to update, edit, or adjust inventory stock quantities.

Parameters:
- `item_code`: Unique SKU, barcode, or item code of the product.
- `actual_stock`: Actual physical stock count (must be a number >= 0).
- `location`: Target inventory location: 'toko' (default) or 'gudang'.
- `reason`: Optional reason or audit note for the stock adjustment.
