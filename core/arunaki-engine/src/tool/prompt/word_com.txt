Automates Windows Microsoft Word via COM for desktop document editing, styling, and formatting.

Usage:
- Parameters:
  * `file`: Relative path to the .docx document.
  * `action`: "open", "append", "replace", "format".
  * `text`: Text content to append or insert.
  * `paragraphIndex`: 0-indexed paragraph number for targeted replacement or styling.
  * `findText` & `replaceText`: Precise substring replacement across the document.
  * Formatting: `bold`, `fontSize`, `alignment` ("left", "center", "right").

CRITICAL RULES:
- Preserve document typography and heading hierarchy.
- Do not erase headers, footers, or unreferenced document sections.
- When replacing text or updating placeholders, ensure neighboring styling and alignment remain intact.
- All operations are strictly bound to the active workspace project folder.
