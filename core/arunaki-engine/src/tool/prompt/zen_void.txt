Void (permanently cancel and delete) an existing sales invoice in ArunakiZen.

Capabilities:
- Cancels the invoice, restores item quantities to store inventory, and reverts cash drawer inflows.
- Requires supervisor authorization (`supervisor_user` and `supervisor_password`).
- Use `arunakizen_edit_sale_order` instead when modifying or revising items on an existing invoice.

Parameters:
- `invoice` (required): The invoice number to void.
- `reason` (optional): Reason for voiding.
- `operator` (optional): Operator name.
- `supervisor_user` (optional): Supervisor username/code.
- `supervisor_password` (optional): Supervisor password.
