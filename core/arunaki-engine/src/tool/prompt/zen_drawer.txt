Send a drawer kick command (hex 1B 70 00 19 FA) to open the physical store cash register drawer.

Capabilities:
- Kicks open the store's hardware cash drawer instantly without requiring a receipt print job.
- Dispatches hardware kick pulse to default or designated thermal receipt printer.

When to use:
- The user requests to open the cash drawer physically (e.g. "open drawer", "kick cash drawer", "open cash register").
