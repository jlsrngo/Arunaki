Perform a 3-Way Reconciliation and Administrative Anomaly/Fraud Audit across workspace documents or provided financial records.
As an Administration Manager, use this tool to cross-verify records, detect financial discrepancies, identify duplicate invoices, and validate mathematical totals.

Supported reconciliation modes (`mode`):
- `cash_and_bank`: Reconciles POS Register Sales <-> Daily Cash Ledger <-> Bank Statement / Vault Deposit.
- `procurement_three_way`: Matches Purchase Order (PO) <-> Delivery Receipt (GRN) <-> Vendor Invoice.

Anomaly & Fraud checks performed automatically:
1. Nominal Variance Detection: Identifies exact monetary discrepancies between sources.
2. Duplicate Invoice / Transaction Guard: Flags repeated invoice numbers or duplicate billing amounts.
3. Calculation Integrity: Validates that line items, taxes, and discounts sum accurately to the stated grand total.
4. Price Deviation Alert: Detects unit price spikes compared to baseline purchase orders.

Generates `reconciliation_audit_report.xlsx` and `reconciliation_discrepancy_memo.docx` in the active workspace and opens them in the Center Panel.
